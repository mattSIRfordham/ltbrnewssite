About Lightbridge

Advancing clean power
toward a brighter future.

Headquartered in Reston, Virginia, Lightbridge Corporation is advancing state-of-the-art fuel technology with the potential to transform the safety, economics and efficiency of nuclear power plants — and to help meet the world's surging demand for reliable, zero-carbon energy.

Key highlights of Lightbridge Fuel™

Better power, by design

Large market

Designed to work in both new and existing reactors — a $20 billion-plus opportunity.

Waste reduction

Less spent fuel per unit of power — and spent fuel that is useless for weapons purposes.

Improved safety

An internal temperature ~1,000 °C cooler — no hydrogen gas under design-basis accidents.

Improved economics

Increases power output and extends fuel-cycle length — the lowest cost to add reliable electricity.

The world's energy needs

A global challenge that needs more power

As the world's population rises, so does its energy consumption — with energy demand projected to climb sharply through 2050. China needs to add the equivalent of today's entire U.S. power system by 2040; India needs to add a power system the size of today's European Union. The challenge is meeting that demand without emitting significantly more carbon.

The world can only achieve its energy and climate goals if nuclear power is a major part of the solution. With Lightbridge Fuel™, nuclear plants can scale more easily — with high availability, better economics and safer operation.

Unique features of the fuel

More power at a lower temperature

With Lightbridge Fuel™, a reactor can produce more power at a lower temperature — thanks to an increased coolant flow rate and increased fuel surface area. By removing flow-restricting spacer grids, more water moves through the core without more pump power, extracting more heat and generating more electricity while improving safety margins.
