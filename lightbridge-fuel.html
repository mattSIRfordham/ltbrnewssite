The Technology

More power.
The same reactors. Engineered differently.

Lightbridge Fuel™ is a proprietary, next-generation metallic fuel designed to improve the economics, safety and proliferation resistance of existing and new reactors — delivering more electricity at a lower cost than any other way of adding baseload power.

Cooler by design

Run cooler. Extract more. Deliver more.

With Lightbridge Fuel™, a reactor can produce more power at a lower temperature. Two features make it possible: an increased coolant flow rate and increased fuel surface area. Remove the flow-restricting spacer grids and more water moves through the core without more pump power — extracting more heat and generating more electricity.

  • Absence of spacer grids may reduce core pressure drop by up to 50%, helping enable power uprates.
  • 35% more fuel surface area increases safety margins and lets the fuel generate more heat.
  • A helically-twisted multi-lobe shape improves coolant mixing and heat transfer.
How we design safer fuel

Engineered at every stage

Fabrication

Three components are metallurgically bonded during fabrication — improving rod integrity and thermal conductivity, and reducing potential radiation exposure for plant workers in the event of a barrier breach.

Shape

A helical multi-lobe rod increases fuel surface area and shortens the distance heat travels to the coolant, improving coolability. Swelling occurs in the valleys between the lobes.

Materials

A central displacer for neutronics control, a uranium-zirconium fuel core with high thermal conductivity, and a corrosion-resistant zirconium-niobium bonded barrier for added protection.

Operations

A low fuel operating temperature means fission products behave like solids and stay where they are created — no fission product release during design-basis events.

Key benefits

Power, safety, savings, security

Efficiency

Existing and new reactors operate at significantly lower temperatures while extracting more heat and delivering greater electricity output — existing PWRs can achieve 10% power uprates and extend fuel cycles from 18 to 24 months.

Safety

Center-rod temperatures over 1,000 °C cooler than standard fuel, with 35% more surface area, more margin to fuel failure, more active-cooling time, and no hydrogen generation under design-basis accidents.

Savings

Using Lightbridge Fuel™, U.S. nuclear plant operators could expect significant internal rates of return after incremental fuel costs and royalties — a high-ROI path to more generation.

Proliferation resistance

Initial enrichment of 15–20% carries the lowest strategic value, with significantly less plutonium than conventional fuel and spent fuel that is useless for weapons purposes.

Capacity factor

Nuclear already runs hardest. We help it run harder.

Nuclear leads every major energy source on capacity factor — the U.S. fleet runs at about 92.7%. Lightbridge Fuel™ is designed to push utilization even further through longer fuel cycles and quicker power ramp-rates that can enable load-follow operation on a renewables-rich grid.

  • Longer fuel cycles — extending from 18 to 24 months eliminates one refueling outage every six years.
  • Quicker ramp-up — like other metallic fuels, it is expected to accommodate rapid power adjustments for load-following.
