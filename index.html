Who we are

The leading fuel technology for a power-hungry planet

Lightbridge (NASDAQ: LTBR) is an advanced nuclear fuel technology company based in Reston, Virginia. We are developing Lightbridge Fuel™ — a proprietary, next-generation metallic fuel for current and future reactors that enhances the economics, safety and proliferation resistance of nuclear power while operating about 1,000 °C cooler than standard fuel.

  • More power per plant — substantial increases to reactor output and economics.
  • Fuel for today and tomorrow — works in large numbers of existing reactors and most reactors planned or under construction.
  • Validated at U.S. National Laboratories — irradiation testing underway at Idaho National Laboratory.
Get to know Lightbridge
Why it matters

A step-change in how the world makes power

Lightbridge Fuel™ is designed to deliver step-change improvements in safety, performance, plant economics, waste reduction and proliferation resistance compared with the industry-standard fuel used today.

More power, more revenue

Increases power output and extends fuel-cycle length — potentially the lowest-cost way to add reliable, zero-carbon electricity to the grid in existing reactors.

Engineered for safety

An internal temperature roughly 1,000 °C cooler than conventional fuel, with no hydrogen generation under design-basis accidents and more time to restore cooling.

Clean & secure

Less spent fuel per unit of power, and spent fuel that is useless for weapons purposes — clean baseload power with stronger non-proliferation credentials.

Better economics

More power generated from the same reactor — designed to deliver high returns for operators of existing and new reactors.

Load-following ready

A quicker ramp-rate may enable load-follow operation on grids rich in renewables — potentially displacing natural gas and coal peaking plants.

Drop-in for the fleet

Designed to work in both new and existing reactors — a $20 billion-plus market of large numbers of reactors that can benefit from Lightbridge Fuel™.

Studsvik partnership

Irradiation testing commenced

Began irradiation testing of enriched uranium-zirconium fuel samples at Idaho National Laboratory's Advanced Test Reactor — a major step toward fuel qualification.
