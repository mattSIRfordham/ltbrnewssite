Company Information

Company profile

Lightbridge Corporation (NASDAQ: LTBR) is a leading developer of advanced nuclear fuel technology for current and future reactors. Lightbridge Fuel™ is designed to enhance the safety, economics and proliferation resistance of nuclear power — operating roughly 1000°C cooler than standard fuel while enabling power uprates and extended cycle lengths.

Headquartered in Reston, Virginia, Lightbridge is advancing its fuel toward commercialization through ongoing testing at U.S. National Laboratories, a worldwide patent platform and strategic industry collaborations — all from a debt-free balance sheet.

FAQ

Investor FAQ

Lightbridge Corporation's common stock trades on the Nasdaq Capital Market under the symbol LTBR. The Company was added to the Russell 2000® and Russell 3000® indexes effective June 30, 2025.

Lightbridge is a leading developer of advanced nuclear fuel technology for current and future reactors. Lightbridge Fuel™ is designed to enhance the safety, economics and proliferation resistance of nuclear power — operating roughly 1000°C cooler than standard fuel, enabling power uprates and extended cycle lengths, and producing less plutonium than standard fuel.

As of March 31, 2026, Lightbridge held approximately $215.7 million in cash and cash equivalents, total assets of approximately $218.9 million, and no debt on its balance sheet — a capital position that supports the Company's development roadmap.
